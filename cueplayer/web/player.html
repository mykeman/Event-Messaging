Cue list

Q-SYS (or anything else) triggers cues by number or label, e.g. CUE 3 over TCP or GET /api/cue/3. A cue with several media items plays them as a sequence.

Normal playlist

The fallback: plays whenever no scenario or event cue is active, after a cue finishes, and when Normal is pressed (which also clears a locked scenario).
