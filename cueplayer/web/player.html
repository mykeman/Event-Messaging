Cue list

Q-SYS (or anything else) triggers cues by number or label, e.g. CUE 3 over TCP or GET /api/cue/3. A cue with several media items plays them as a sequence.

#LabelMediaLoop DurationThenColour

Default content

Plays whenever nothing has been triggered, and after a cue finishes (unless the cue says otherwise).

Output layout

Blue = canvas (what NDI carries). Dashed = the physical display raster. Use the offset to map the canvas onto the region your LED processor captures.
